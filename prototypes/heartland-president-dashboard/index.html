How every number is calculated

One definition per KPI. Where a measure also appears on the CFO dashboard it is the same definition and the same number — net revenue is one figure in this company, not two.

Where the numbers come from
What we shipped + what we spent + what it cost to serve = one commercial conversation

What this draft is asking you to react to

  • One dashboard for two chairs. The President's question is "is the growth real and can we serve it"; marketing's is "did the money work". They share the same numbers, so they share a page. Tell us if that holds or if marketing needs its own view.
  • Channel is the only global control. Not entity, not brand. Dollar measures rescale per channel; product-line and portfolio measures stay consolidated and say so. Measures that do not exist in a channel — retailer OTIF fines in Foodservice, promotion mechanics in Foodservice — say that instead of showing a zero.
  • The growth decomposition is the centrepiece. Volume, price, mix, new items, lost distribution. Everything else on the Month tab exists to explain one of those five bars.
  • Phase 1 can tell you what we shipped and what we spent. It cannot tell you what the shopper did. Share, %ACV, true promotion lift and brand health are dashed panels until NIQ and the retailer POS portals land. We would rather show the gap than fill it with a proxy.
  • Numbers are illustrative and tie to the CFO draft wherever the two overlap. The shapes, definitions and drill paths are the proposal.
  • Open question for kickoff: what is the service number you promise your top five customers, and who owns it? Fill rate and OTIF are on three of the four tabs here because nothing else on this page survives a supply failure — but the target and the owner are yours to name.
