Three dashboards, one set of numbers.

Each of these is built for one chair, on one cadence structure: today, this week, this month, this quarter. Each takes a single question and answers it before the coffee is cold. What makes them a set rather than three reports is that where a measure appears on more than one, it is the same measure with the same definition and the same value — net revenue is one figure in this company, not three.

What Phase 1 cannot answer

Three gaps, shown as dashed panels rather than filled with a proxy

Every dashboard in this set can say what we shipped and what we spent. None of them can say what the shopper or the operator did. Rather than substitute a proxy and let it quietly become the truth, each view marks the gap where the answer belongs.

  • Market share, %ACV and promotion lift — needs NIQ syndicated data. Whether our 7.8% growth was share gained or a rising tide is not in any Phase 1 source, and neither is what a promotion actually bought.
  • Foodservice sell-through — needs distributor POS. We know what we invoiced to US Foods and Sysco; when a distributor moves inventory, sell-in moves and demand does not. The Foodservice forecast runs on shipment patterns until this lands.
  • Brand health — needs a consumer panel and a brand tracker. The only place brand spend can be shown to do something trade spend cannot.

Reading these in a review

  • Cadence is a tab, not a filter. The time grain is pre-selected on every view, so nobody starts a meeting by choosing a date range.
  • The attention feed is the product. Each tab ranks at most five exceptions, with a severity stripe plus an icon and a text label — never colour alone. Everything above the feed is context.
  • Every number carries one comparison — prior period, plan, forecast, budget, last year, or the customer's own requirement — plus a sparkline.
  • Nothing is gated behind a hover. Every chart has a Table toggle that swaps the graphic for the same numbers.
  • Drill-down is one click, never required. Tiles and alerts marked Detail → open the underlying customer, invoice, order, SKU or territory list.
  • All values are illustrative. The shapes, definitions, comparisons and drill paths are the proposal; the numbers are placeholders sized to a company like Heartland until the marts point at SYSPRO.
