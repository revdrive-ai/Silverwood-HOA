How every number is calculated

One definition per KPI. Shipments, net revenue and trade spend are the same figures as the CFO and President dashboards — a territory number that does not roll up to the company number is worse than no number.

Where the numbers come from
What shipped + what we said would ship + what it cost to get there = a forecast call you can defend

What this draft is asking you to react to

  • Two global controls, not one. The CFO and President views each take a single control on purpose. This one takes territory and customer, because that is how you actually work — you manage people by territory and revenue by account. The customer list narrows to the territory you pick, and picking a customer tells you which territory owns it.
  • Every scorecard sorts. Click any column header — territory, customer, attainment, trade rate, growth, fill. Sort order is yours per table; nothing is locked to a house ranking.
  • Forecast is a separate number from plan. Plan is the annual commitment; forecast is the call your team is making now. Attainment against both is on every tile, and forecast accuracy by territory says whose number you can take to the boardroom.
  • Foodservice has its own block, not just a filter value. Distributor mix, operator segments, contract deviations rather than retail promotion mechanics, and the sell-in versus sell-through gap. It is also selectable in the territory picker, since it has its own sales force.
  • The honest gap: we can see sell-in, not sell-through. We know what we shipped to US Foods and Sysco. What the operator actually bought needs distributor POS data, and until that lands the Foodservice forecast is built on shipment patterns rather than demand. That is the dashed panel on the Quarter tab, and it is the single biggest blind spot on this page.
  • Open question for kickoff: at what point in the month does a rep's forecast lock? Everything on the weekly tab — revision tracking, at-risk revenue, forecast accuracy — needs one agreed cut-off, and that is a sales-management decision rather than a data one.
